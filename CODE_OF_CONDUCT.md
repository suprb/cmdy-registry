# Community code of conduct

We want the cmdy community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting useful
feedback, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy
toward other contributors. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, publishing another
person's private information, or sustained disruption of project work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct concerns to
a maintainer through a private channel. Maintainers will respect the privacy
and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project in public spaces.
